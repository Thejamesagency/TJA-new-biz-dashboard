Conversations setup

The Conversations page reads from crm@thejamesagency.com — the shared archive mailbox you already CC on client email chains.

Matching logic: a thread shows up under a client if the subject line contains the client's name (case-insensitive), or any of the manually-added subject aliases for that client.

Two ways data lands here:
A. Manual paste — click "+ Paste email" on any client. Works now, no backend required.
B. Auto-pull from crm@ — requires the Cloud Function backend (service account + domain-wide delegation). Once deployed, click "↻ Refresh" on any client.
