How it could work

1

Calendars sync Monday

Google Calendar free/busy pulls in automatically at the start of the week (meeting titles stay private — just “busy”).

2

SMEs set their rhythm

Add email-check windows + focus blocks once; “repeat every weekday” means most people set it and forget it.

3

AM/PMs plan around it

Before promising a client a turnaround, check who's reachable and when. Could also tie to the Resourcing Cockpit load so heavy weeks are obvious.

Open questions for the team

  • Would SMEs actually keep it current? Only works if it's ~1 min a week.
  • What counts as urgent? Is there an override (e.g. Slack) for true client fires?
  • Minimum email checks? e.g. a team norm of 2–3 windows a day.
  • Calendar privacy — busy blocks only, or show meeting names?
  • Where it lives — here, or alongside the resourcing tools in the internal dashboard?
